## Legal and provider evidence boundaries

- The audit identifies issues requiring review, not a legal certification.
- The laws relevant to privacy, cookies, age thresholds, and physical addresses depend on the audience, jurisdiction, data practices, and commercial model.
- Provider dashboard state must be observed. Static code analysis does not prove protection, backups, spending policy, logs, or live traffic health.
